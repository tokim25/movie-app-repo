      About Family Feature

      Family Feature combines the movie catalog, Common Sense Media age guidance, your watched and Want to watch marks, and each child's content settings.

      • Tonight checks the selected kids first. Adults-only nights do not apply child safety limits.
      • Recommendations pull from Want to watch first, recent releases second, and the rest of Shelf last.
      • The kind of night changes the order inside those pools: calm favors lower energy and lower intensity, silly favors comedy and animation, something new favors recent release years, and old favourite favors watched titles.
      • Each movie gets an experience profile built from its age guidance, genre, release year, description, watched state, and Want to watch state.
      • Age-based defaults are set automatically from a child's age. Parent-set limits stay fixed unless you switch them back to the age-based default.
      • CSM means Common Sense Media. DCOM means Disney Channel Original Movie.

      Your family data stays in this browser unless you turn on sync. Google sync stores a small app-only file in your Google Drive.
